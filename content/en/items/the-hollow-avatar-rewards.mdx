## What avatar rewards can you earn in The Hollow?

Looking for The Hollow avatar rewards before the event ends? Roblox says completing quests earns exclusive user-generated content (UGC), alongside rewards that can help players survive. It has not published a quest-by-quest list of UGC items in its [official announcement of The Hollow](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/10/the-hollow-halloween-survival-event), so don’t assume a particular avatar item is a quest reward without checking its in-event requirements.

## How do you earn quest rewards?

Enter The Hollow’s event hub and work on its quests. Roblox describes a changing maze where players search for clues and avoid a new monster each day. Completing quests is the confirmed way to earn exclusive UGC and survival-related rewards, but the announcement does not specify which quests award which items.

Players can continue collecting rewards until **noon PST on November 2, 2026**, according to Roblox. Check the event hub for the requirements of any reward you want before that deadline.

## Which avatar items are sold separately?

Roblox also announced limited-time Halloween avatar items **for sale on Marketplace**. These include the Hollow Horseman, dozens of exclusive avatar items, and seasonal makeup looks. The announcement does not identify these Marketplace items as quest prizes or provide their prices.

If you’re deciding where to spend your time, keep the distinction clear: **quests earn event UGC and other rewards; Marketplace items are sold separately.**

## What do Roblox Plus subscribers get?

Active Roblox Plus subscribers received early access to The Hollow’s hub ahead of the public opening. Roblox also announced an exclusive profile frame and background, plus an item launcher subscribers can use to gift items to other players during the event. An active Plus subscription allows players to keep the event theme beyond the finale.

These subscriber perks are separate from the quest-earned UGC. Roblox does not describe the profile frame or background as avatar items.

## FAQ

### Are The Hollow avatar rewards free?

Roblox confirms that players can earn exclusive UGC by completing quests, but its announcement does not list individual items or every requirement. It separately identifies Halloween avatar items on Marketplace as being for sale.

### Is the Hollow Horseman a quest reward?

Roblox lists the Hollow Horseman among limited-time Marketplace avatar items for sale, not among named quest rewards.

### When is the last chance to collect The Hollow rewards?

Roblox says players can collect rewards until **noon PST on November 2, 2026**.
