## The Hollow at a Glance

Nine familiar horror-game monsters are entering The Hollow, giving players a new threat to evade each day. If you want to recognize who you’re facing, the key is knowing which game each monster comes from. Roblox has confirmed all nine pairings, but its announcement does not assign a specific date or appearance order to each monster.

## The Hollow Monsters and Participating Games

Roblox names the following monsters and games in its [official announcement of The Hollow Halloween event](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/10/the-hollow-halloween-survival-event):

| Monster | Participating game |
|---|---|
| The Deer | 99 Nights in the Forest |
| Seek | DOORS |
| Twisted Dandy | Dandy’s World |
| Piggy | Piggy |
| The Inspector | Scary Shawarma Kiosk |
| Mimic | Animal Hospital |
| Biter | Demonology |
| Hiachi | The Mimic |
| The Overseer | Warden |

These are the announced pairings, not a confirmed day-by-day lineup. In particular, **Mimic** is listed as coming from *Animal Hospital*, while **Hiachi** comes from the game *The Mimic*.

## How the Monsters Fit Into the Event

The Hollow is a shared survival event set in an event hub. During its nine-day monster rollout, players encounter a new maze each day, look for clues, and evade monsters. Roblox says players can complete quests to earn exclusive user-generated content (UGC) and rewards that may help them survive another night.

The announcement does not detail each monster’s abilities or explain how encounters differ from the monsters’ original games. Familiarity with a participating game may help you recognize a character, but it should not be treated as a confirmed strategy for The Hollow.

## Event Dates and Access

The Hollow opened on **October 22, 2026, at 2 p.m. PT**. Roblox announced a live finale for the morning of **October 31**, with reward collection continuing until **noon PST on November 2**.

Roblox says the event is available to eligible Roblox Select or Roblox account holders, generally ages 9 and older, though age and access rules vary by region. Active Roblox Plus subscribers were offered early hub access at noon PT on opening day; a Plus subscription is not listed as a requirement for general access.

## FAQ

### What are The Hollow monsters and participating games?

The Hollow features nine announced pairings: The Deer from *99 Nights in the Forest*, Seek from *DOORS*, Twisted Dandy from *Dandy’s World*, Piggy from *Piggy*, The Inspector from *Scary Shawarma Kiosk*, Mimic from *Animal Hospital*, Biter from *Demonology*, Hiachi from *The Mimic*, and The Overseer from *Warden*.

### Is the order of the nine monsters confirmed?

No. Roblox says a different monster appears each day during the nine-day rollout, but its announcement does not specify which monster appears on which date.

### Can players still collect rewards after the finale?

Yes. Although the live finale is scheduled for October 31, Roblox says players can continue collecting rewards until noon PST on November 2.
