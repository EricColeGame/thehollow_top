## The Hollow event dates at a glance

The Hollow is already underway, but its Halloween finale and reward deadline are still ahead. Roblox says the event opened on October 22, 2026, its live finale begins the morning of October 31, and players can keep collecting rewards until **noon PST on November 2**. If you are planning around the event, that last deadline matters more than the finale date.

| Milestone | Confirmed timing |
| --- | --- |
| Early hub access for active Roblox Plus subscribers | October 22 at noon PT |
| Event opens to eligible players | October 22 at 2 p.m. PT |
| Live finale begins | Morning of October 31 |
| Reward collection ends | November 2 at noon PST |

These timings come from [Roblox's official announcement of The Hollow](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/10/the-hollow-halloween-survival-event). Roblox does not give a specific start time for the October 31 finale.

## What happens between the opening and finale?

The Hollow is a nine-day survival event built around a changing maze. Roblox says a new monster enters each day as players search for clues, complete quests, and earn rewards. The announcement names all nine featured monsters, but it does **not** assign each one a date, so there is no confirmed day-by-day monster schedule in the source.

The live finale starts on October 31. It is not the final opportunity to collect rewards: Roblox says that window remains open until noon PST on November 2.

## Who can join The Hollow?

Roblox says The Hollow is available to eligible Roblox and Roblox Select account holders. The event is intended for players ages 9 and older in most regions, though age and access rules vary by region.

Active Roblox Plus subscribers received early access to explore the hub at noon PT on opening day, two hours before the general opening. That early-access window does not change the November 2 reward deadline.

## FAQ

### When did The Hollow start?

The Hollow opened to eligible players on **October 22, 2026, at 2 p.m. PT**. Active Roblox Plus subscribers could enter the hub starting at noon PT that day.

### When is The Hollow's live finale?

Roblox says the live finale begins **the morning of October 31, 2026**. It has not specified an exact time in the cited announcement.

### Do The Hollow event dates end on Halloween?

No. The live finale is on October 31, but players can continue collecting rewards until **noon PST on November 2, 2026**.

### Is there a confirmed schedule for each monster?

Roblox confirms that a different monster enters The Hollow each day for nine days. Its announcement does not specify which monster appears on each date.
