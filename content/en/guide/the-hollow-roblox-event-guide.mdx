## How to play The Hollow

The Hollow changes each day, so a route that worked yesterday may not help today. This Roblox Halloween event puts players in a changing maze to search for clues, avoid monsters, and complete quests for rewards. You can still collect rewards after the Oct. 31 live finale, but the event’s stated cutoff is noon PST on Nov. 2, 2026.

Enter The Hollow event hub with an eligible Roblox account, then check the current maze for clues and available quests. Roblox says the event is intended for players ages 9 and older in most regions; access rules vary by region. The [official Roblox announcement for The Hollow](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/10/the-hollow-halloween-survival-event) does not provide a fixed maze route or quest-by-quest walkthrough, so check the objectives shown in the event rather than relying on a route for a previous day.

## What changes each day?

The Hollow is one continuous survival event, but Roblox says players encounter a new maze and a different monster each day across nine days. That makes the current day’s clues and threats the useful starting point whenever you enter the hub.

Roblox has named all nine featured monsters:

- The Deer from *99 Nights in the Forest*
- Seek from *DOORS*
- Twisted Dandy from *Dandy’s World*
- Piggy from *Piggy*
- The Inspector from *Scary Shawarma Kiosk*
- Mimic from *Animal Hospital*
- Biter from *Demonology*
- Hiachi from *The Mimic*
- The Overseer from *Warden*

The announcement names the roster but does not assign a date to each monster or explain its behavior inside The Hollow. Familiarity with its original game may help you recognize it, but do not assume the same mechanics apply here.

## How do you earn rewards?

Complete quests in The Hollow to earn exclusive user-generated content (UGC) and other rewards that Roblox says can help you survive later nights. The announcement does not list individual quests, reward requirements, or a complete prize catalog. For a practical next step, review the quests available in the hub and prioritize any rewards you want before collection closes.

Marketplace Halloween avatar items are separate from the quest rewards. Roblox says limited-time items for sale include the Hollow Horseman, other exclusive avatar items, and seasonal makeup looks. The announcement does not provide their prices.

## When is the finale and deadline?

The Hollow opened to players at 2 p.m. Pacific time on Oct. 22, 2026. Roblox says the live finale begins on the **morning of Oct. 31**, but its announcement does not give an exact start time. Reward collection continues until **noon PST on Nov. 2**.

Roblox Plus subscribers received early hub access on opening day, along with subscriber extras including a profile frame, background, and an item launcher for gifting during the event. Those extras are not described as requirements for joining the event.

## FAQ

### Is The Hollow Roblox event guide based on a confirmed maze solution?

No. Roblox confirms that the maze changes daily, but its announcement does not provide routes or puzzle solutions. Use the clues and quests available in the current version of the hub.

### Do I need Roblox Plus to play The Hollow?

No. Roblox describes the event as available to eligible account holders. Plus provides additional perks, including early access on opening day, rather than being a stated requirement to play.

### Does The Hollow end with the Oct. 31 finale?

The live finale is on Oct. 31, but Roblox says players can continue collecting rewards until noon PST on Nov. 2, 2026.

### What age do players need to be?

Roblox says The Hollow is aimed at players ages 9 and older in most regions. Age and access rules may differ by region.
